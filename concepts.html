Distance

Distance in striking is a river.

The water is deep and moving. Both banks are open. That does not make the crossing safe. It only means you can step in whenever you like — and get taken by the current if you guess.

Before you enter, you survey. Where it shallows. Where the line is short. That surveying is the skill. The crossing is the fight.

Danger is that stretch of water between you and them. Too far and you only watch. Too soon and you wade the deep part on their terms. Sloppy entry slows you. Sometimes it puts you under.

This is why we use light contact. You get to survey the same river again and again without losing the army — which here is just you. See. Choose the ford. Cross on purpose.
